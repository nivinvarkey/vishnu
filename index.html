About me

I'm a mechanical engineer with 10 years of experience in oil and gas EPC projects, structural steel and fabrication design, and manufacturing.

As Pipe Support Lead at EPIC Piping International, I deliver GA, detail and fabrication drawings and material take-offs for onshore and offshore projects for ADNOC, Saudi Aramco, NPCC, Saipem, Tecnimont and JGC. I manage review and revision cycles, approve final deliverables, control client transmittals and coordinate with engineering, fabrication and QA/QC teams.

Before that I designed PVC pipe production equipment, manufacturing machinery and pre-engineered buildings, and started my career as an NDT inspector. I've been drafting since school, with engineering graphics from Class VIII in technical education. I'm certified in structural steel detailing and modelling with Tekla Structures.
